2. What the App does, and what it doesn't promise

Tracking, punch counts and scores are estimates made by software. They can miss or miscount punches and are not exact measurements. Boxmode is a training aid, not a personal coach, a medical device or a substitute for professional instruction. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. We don't promise any particular result, such as fitness, weight loss, strength, skill or "a fighter's body"; results depend on you.

The App needs a compatible iPhone and enough light and space. AI analysis and subscriptions need an internet connection. AI analysis has a daily limit (currently 5 analyses a day per installation) and can occasionally be unavailable.

3. Health and safety

Boxing training is physical exercise and can cause injury. The App does not give medical advice.

  • Check with a doctor before you start, especially if you have a heart, blood-pressure, breathing, joint or back condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury or illness.
  • Warm up before you train, and train at a level that suits you.
  • Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, shortness of breath, chest pain or you feel unwell, and get medical help if needed.
  • Clear the space around you. Keep people, pets, furniture, walls, glass and ceiling fans out of reach, train on a floor that isn't slippery, and prop your iPhone on a steady stand, never in your hand.
  • Boxmode is for shadowboxing only. Never hit people, animals, walls or objects while using it.

You're responsible for training safely within your own limits. Nothing in this section limits our liability where the law doesn't allow it (section 11).

4. AI analysis

AI analysis is optional and only runs after you allow it in the App. It uses a third-party AI model (Google's Gemini, through OpenRouter). Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent, to whom, and for how long.

AI feedback is created by a machine. It can be wrong, incomplete or inconsistent, and the same round can get different feedback. Scores are estimates, not objective measurements. The feedback is about boxing technique only. It is not medical, injury or safety advice, and it doesn't tell you whether a movement is safe for you. Check every tip against the moment in your clip and use your own judgment.
