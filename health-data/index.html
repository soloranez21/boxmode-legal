Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Effective: October 23, 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, whose laws protect "consumer health data". It adds to our Privacy Policy, which explains everything else.

Boxmode is a boxing coach for iPhone, made by Aleksandr Saliukov ("we", "us"), Goeschlgasse 3, 1030 Vienna, Austria, support@glaw.app.

What we treat as consumer health data

Boxmode is a fitness app, not a health or medical app. Almost everything it measures stays on your iPhone, and we never receive it. That includes the live camera picture, the body points the app tracks, your rounds and history, and your setup answers such as your goals.

Only one feature sends data to us: AI round analysis, and only after you allow it. Some of what it sends could count as consumer health data under these laws, so we treat it that way:

  1. Video and still pictures of your body and face from the clip you analyze. Pictures of a face can count as biometric data, even though we never create face or body templates or identify anyone.
  2. Movement information measured on your iPhone and sent with the clip: when each punch happened and its type, moments when both hands were low, and how much of your body was visible. Also your stance and experience level.
  3. The AI report about your technique: tips, one drill and scores from 1 to 99 for guard, punches, defense, footwork, combos and pace.

How we use it, and why

We use this data only to give you the AI analysis you asked for: our server receives the upload, sends it through OpenRouter to Google's Gemini model, and returns the report to your iPhone. We don't use it for anything else. We don't use it for advertising, we make no decisions about you with it, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models. Boxmode doesn't use your location, so it never uses geofences.

Where it comes from

From you, through the Boxmode app on your iPhone: the clip you record or pick from your photos, the measurements the app makes on your iPhone, and the answers you gave in setup. The AI report is created by Google's Gemini model from that data.

Who receives it

We never sell consumer health data, and we have no affiliates. We disclose it only to these processors, which handle it for us under contract and only to provide the analysis:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs our server and stores the upload while the analysis runs. Contact: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • OpenRouter, Inc. passes the request to the AI model. Contact: openrouter.ai/privacy
  • Google LLC runs the Gemini model and processes the request for OpenRouter. Contact: policies.google.com/privacy

The video, pictures and movement information (1 and 2) go to all three. The AI report (3) is created by Google and comes back to your iPhone through OpenRouter and our server.

No other company, and no company that collects data across different websites or apps, receives consumer health data through Boxmode.

Your consent

We collect this data only with your consent: before the first upload, Boxmode explains what would be sent and asks you to tap "Allow AI analysis". You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → "Allow uploads for AI analysis". We will never collect, use or disclose consumer health data in a new way, for a new purpose or to a new recipient without telling you first and asking for your consent.

How long we keep it

  • The upload (video, pictures and movement information) is deleted from our server as soon as the analysis ends, and an automatic rule deletes anything left after 1 day.
  • The AI report stays on our server for at most 1 hour so your iPhone can collect it. After that it's only on your iPhone.
  • Deleted reports and records can stay in our hosting provider's recovery logs for up to 30 days before they're erased for good. We don't restore them.
  • OpenRouter keeps request details such as time and cost for billing, and may keep the content only as its privacy policy and our account settings allow.
  • Google keeps the request and the report for up to 55 days, only to detect misuse of its service.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of every third party and affiliate that received it (the processors above, with their contact pages);
  • withdraw your consent (Settings → "Allow uploads for AI analysis");
  • have it deleted. We delete it from our server and tell our processors to delete it too;
  • ask us to stop collecting or sharing it.

The quickest way to delete is in the app: Settings → "Delete all my data" deletes everything on your iPhone and our server's records right away. You can also email support@glaw.app with the subject "Consumer health data request". Because Boxmode has no accounts and our server holds no name or contact details, we may not be able to match an email to server data; it is deleted automatically within the times above anyway.

We answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this once by 45 days and tell you why. Using your rights is free and never changes how Boxmode treats you. You can see your AI reports in the app at any time; to change one, delete it and analyze the clip again.

Appeals. If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying "Appeal" to our answer. We answer within 45 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general (in Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update the dates above and publish the new version at this address before it takes effect. If a change would let us use consumer health data in a new way, we ask for your consent in the app first.
