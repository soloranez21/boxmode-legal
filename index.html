Help and legal

Boxmode is a boxing coach for iPhone.

Questions? Email us: support@glaw.app

Quick help

  • Cancel your subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Boxmode: Settings → "Manage subscription".
  • Restore a purchase: in Boxmode: Settings → "Restore purchases".
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Turn AI analysis off: in Boxmode: Settings → "Allow uploads for AI analysis".
  • Delete your data: in Boxmode: Settings → "Delete all my data".
